§ 03 — Core Features
Six features · Tubelens
01
One-click Import
Drop your Google Takeout .zip. Tubelens extracts watch history, subscriptions, likes, playlists, comments, and searches — zero configuration.
02
Visual Dashboard
Calendar heatmap, KPI cards, and top-channels ranking built with Recharts and Server Components. Dark-mode native.
03
AI-Powered Insights
Claude Haiku analyzes your patterns and surfaces binge sessions, topic clusters, and time-of-day habits in plain language.
04
Subscription Mismatch
Cross-reference what you subscribe to vs. what you actually watch. Surfaces channels you should unsubscribe from.
05
History Explorer
Filterable, searchable watch history with enriched video and channel metadata from YouTube Data API v3.
06
Privacy-First
All data lives in your own Neon Postgres instance, scoped to your login. Nothing is shared. Delete any time.

§ 04 — How It Works
5 steps · Tubelens
From raw export to self-knowledge
1
Export
Go to Google Takeout → select YouTube → download .zip archive.
2
Upload
Drop the zip into Tubelens. JSZip + PapaParse + Cheerio extract every CSV and HTML file.
3
Enrich
Route Handlers call YouTube Data API v3 to enrich channels and videos with metadata.
4
Analyze
Claude Haiku generates Insight records — binge patterns, topic clusters, peak hours.
5
Discover
Server Components render your dashboard — no client-side fetch, instant data on load.

§ 05 · AI Insights · Tubelens
What Claude surfaces
Binge Sessions
Clusters of 5+ consecutive videos within 2 hours — how often, what topics, which time of day.
Topic Drift
How your interests shifted month-by-month. "You went from cooking in Jan to DevOps by March."
Subscription Mismatch
Channels you subscribed to but never returned to watch — unsubscribe candidates.
Peak Hours
Your heaviest watch windows by hour of day, day of week, and month of year.
