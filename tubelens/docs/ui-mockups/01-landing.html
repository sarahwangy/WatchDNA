How It Works

Three steps.
No setup, no accounts linked.

We never connect to your YouTube account. You control the data, we just read it.

01
📦

Download Takeout

Go to takeout.google.com, select YouTube data, and download your archive. Takes about 2 minutes.

02
⬆️

Upload Your File

Drop the watch-history.json file here. We parse it locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device unencrypted.

03
🔍

Read Your Report

Get your full dashboard instantly: heatmaps, category breakdowns, loyalty scores, and an AI-generated portrait of your watch personality.

🔒

Your data never leaves without your permission.

Tubelens processes your watch history on-device for initial parsing. We store only aggregated statistics — never raw video IDs or titles. Your Google Takeout file is deleted from our servers within 24 hours of processing. We don't sell data. We don't train models on your history.
